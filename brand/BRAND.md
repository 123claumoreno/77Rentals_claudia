# 77Rentals brand

The logo, colors, fonts and voice of 77Rentals (77rentals.com). Read this
before making anything branded: a poster, a reel, a listing, a page, a PDF.

The 77rentals.com website code (repo 77Rentals/77Rentals, `BRAND.md`) is the
source of truth. This copy was taken on 2026-11-02; if they disagree, the
website wins.

## Name

- Write it **77Rentals**: one word, capital R. The web address is
  77rentals.com (lowercase).
- Sebastian's AI consulting practice is **77 IA**, at 77rentals.com/ai.

## Logo

- File: `brand/logos/logo77.jpeg`, 661 x 475 px.
- What it shows: a gold house roof with a four-pane window, a gold key whose
  blade runs under the word, "77" and "RENTALS" in white, on a solid purple
  background.
- This JPEG on purple is the only version that exists. There is no SVG, no
  transparent PNG and no one-color version.
  - Place it on a purple field that matches its background, **#3B2055**, so
    the edge disappears, or show it as a rounded card.
  - Don't cut it out, put it straight on a photo, or let a box show around it.
  - Don't redraw it, recolor it, stretch it or regenerate it with AI. If a
    piece needs a vector or transparent version, ask the owner for the
    original file.
- Icons: `brand/logos/favicon.svg` (a simplified gold house on purple),
  `brand/logos/favicon.ico`, `brand/logos/apple-touch-icon.png`. The favicon uses
  #4A1272 and #D4A017, which are close to the palette below but not the same.

## Colors

Purple, gold and white. `brand/colors/palette.css` has them as CSS variables.

| Color | Token | HSL | Hex | Use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Purple | `--primary` | 260 55% 25% | #341D63 | Main brand color, headings, body text, buttons |
| Deep purple | `--purple-dark` | 260 55% 18% | #251547 | Dark sections, hover states |
| Light purple | `--purple-light` | 260 30% 95% | #F1EEF6 | Tinted section backgrounds |
| Gold | `--gold`, `--accent` | 42 55% 55% | #CBA54D | Accents, highlights, icons, details |
| Light gold | `--gold-light` | 42 60% 70% | #E0C585 | Soft accents on dark backgrounds |
| White | `--background` | 0 0% 100% | #FFFFFF | Page background, text on purple |
| Muted text | `--muted-foreground` | 260 10% 45% | #6F677E | Secondary text, captions |
| Border | `--border` | 260 15% 88% | #DFDCE5 | Lines and dividers |

Dark mode: background #110920, cards #190E2F, text #F2F2F2, and gold
becomes the primary color.

Contrast (WCAG):

- White on purple is about 14:1 and gold on purple about 6:1. Both are safe
  for text.
- Gold on white is about 2.3:1. Don't use it for text on white; keep gold
  for shapes, lines and icons there.

## Fonts

- **Headings: Fraunces**, a serif, at weight 400 with optical sizing on.
  Free on Google Fonts: https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Fraunces
- **Body and interface: General Sans** at weights 400, 500 and 600. Free on
  Fontshare: https://www.fontshare.com/fonts/general-sans
- **/ai pages and the printable guide PDFs: Manrope** at weights 500 to 800,
  with print text in #1B1235. https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Manrope

If a tool doesn't have these fonts, fall back to a plain serif for headings
and a plain sans-serif for text. Don't swap in a decorative font.

## Voice and copy

House rules for every piece:

- No em dashes or en dashes as punctuation. Use commas, colons, periods or
  parentheses.
- Spanish uses "tú" and Colombian vocabulary. English is written natively,
  not translated word for word.
- Only verified facts. Never invent results, ratings, reviews or
  testimonials. Date anything time-sensitive.

The call to action is WhatsApp ("Escríbenos por WhatsApp", "Reserva por
WhatsApp"). The rentals line is +57 304 673 6241 (wa.me/573046736241). The AI
consulting line, +57 350 205 3147, is only for 77 IA pieces. Instagram is
@co77rentals.

### Posters and promos

The owner set these rules for the Macondo posters (2026-10-19). Check with
the owner before relaxing them for another unit:

- Don't print the apartment number, prices, ratings or reviews, or the
  host's name.
- Don't claim a beach distance or a sea view unless it's verified for that
  unit.
- Don't use lotería imagery or indigenous patterns (Wayuu, Arhuaco, Kogui,
  mola) as decoration.

### Video

- The narrator never says "77Rentals" or "siete siete". The brand appears on
  screen only: logo, 77rentals.com and WhatsApp on the closing card.
- Close the narration on the call to action or the place name instead.
